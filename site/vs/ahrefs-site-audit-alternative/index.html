Is Ahrefs Site Audit free?

Yes, for sites whose ownership you verify. Ahrefs Free includes 5,000 crawl credits a month per project and unlimited verified sites. A paid plan is needed for sites you cannot verify, such as a prospect's or a competitor's. A client's site can use the free allowance if its ownership is verified.

Docket's free version works on any public site with no account. It runs the audit and shows the score, the number of problems in each area and the top problem with its fix. Crawl credits, page caps and extra charges

A crawl credit pays for one internal HTML page that returns a 200 status. Redirects, pages that return an error, images, CSS and JavaScript files, and links to other sites do not spend credits. Each verified project's first 5,000 credits a month are free on every plan.

Two limits to check before a large crawl

Lite's 100,000 monthly credits do not let one project exceed its 25,000 page cap. Standard caps a project at 50,000 pages, and Advanced at 250,000. Re-crawling pages after a fix uses the monthly allowance again.

Extra pay-as-you-go credits are off by default. If you enable them, Ahrefs' pricing FAQ says you are automatically charged when use exceeds the plan's limits. Bought credits last 3 billing months. No price for extra crawl credits was stated on the pricing page or the credit help pages read on 9 November 2026.

Docket has no monthly credits or per-audit charge. You choose the page and depth settings. A crawl still stops at its deadline, when it runs out of links, or at the limits of the computer's memory. The default deadline is 15 minutes. Everything mode allows 24 hours.

What Ahrefs does that Docket does not

  • Index data and rank tracking. Ahrefs supplies backlink and keyword data, plus rank tracking. Docket has no backlink or keyword index and does not track search rankings. It has no Search Console connection.
  • Cloud scheduling and always-on crawling. Ahrefs lets you schedule crawls by day, time and timezone. Always-on audits are available on Lite and higher. Docket's schedule runs only while the app is open and the computer is awake. It has no cloud or always-on crawler.
  • Paid team seats. Lite includes 1 user and lets you add up to 2 at $40 a month each. Standard allows 5 extra at $60 each, and Advanced 10 at $80 each. Docket has no team accounts or shared workspace.
  • Password-protected crawls. Ahrefs' settings help lists authentication on Advanced and higher. Docket has no setup for logging into a protected site. Use public staging pages or a tool with authentication.
  • JavaScript across the crawl. Ahrefs lets you enable JavaScript rendering and search raw HTML, rendered HTML and page text. Docket renders a sample by default. A bug on an unsampled page may be missed.
  • Crawl speed and scale. Ahrefs advertises up to 170k URLs crawled per minute. We have not benchmarked that claim. Docket's default is 4 concurrent requests with a 0.25-second delay per worker. Large sites take longer on a laptop.
  • Looker Studio. Ahrefs' Site Audit connector needs Advanced or higher and consumes no credits. Docket has no connector.

What Docket adds to the audit workflow

Docket ranks fixes by impact against effort. It checks calls to action, enquiry forms and tracking readiness alongside technical SEO. These checks inspect pages. They do not read your conversion results from an Analytics account.

For AI access, Docket checks robots.txt rules for 22 AI crawlers. A separate optional server test requests the homepage as 9 AI crawlers and Googlebot, with Docket's identity added to each request. That can find a server refusal even when robots.txt allows the crawler. It does not prove the crawler will index or cite the page.

AI crawler server tests, conversion checks and tracking-readiness checks are not stated on Ahrefs' Site Audit page on 9 November 2026. Report branding is not stated on its pricing or Site Audit page on that date. An omitted feature is not proof that Ahrefs cannot do it. Ask Ahrefs if one decides the purchase.

Docket's paid version puts your own name and logo on the client PDF. The audit runs on your computer, and its saved crawl and reports stay there unless you share them. Five optional checks fetch outside data. --offline turns those checks off. The crawler still needs to fetch the site.

Common questions

Is Ahrefs Site Audit free?

Ahrefs Free gives 5,000 crawl credits a month per verified project, with unlimited verified sites. Sites you cannot verify need a paid plan. Read 9 November 2026 on Ahrefs' Free page. Docket's free version works on any public site with no account.

Can I audit a client's site with Ahrefs?

Yes. Paid plans allow 5 unverified projects on Lite, 20 on Standard and 50 on Advanced, per Ahrefs' pricing page read 9 November 2026. If the site's ownership is verified, it can use Ahrefs Free.

What happens when I run out of Ahrefs crawl credits?

Pay-as-you-go extras are off by default. Enabling them permits automatic charges above the plan limits. No extra crawl-credit price was stated on Ahrefs' pricing and credit help pages on 9 November 2026. Docket has no credits, but its crawl has page and depth settings and a 15-minute default deadline.

Is there a one-time alternative to Ahrefs Site Audit?

Docket runs on Mac and 64-bit Windows with no crawl credits. Launch price: $29.50 once, through 30 November. From 1 December: $59. One licence covers any number of sites on up to 3 computers at a time, Mac or Windows.

Does Ahrefs Site Audit render JavaScript?

Yes. Its Site Audit page says it executes JavaScript, with rendering enabled in crawl settings. Read 9 November 2026. Docket renders a sample of pages by default.

Does Ahrefs Site Audit have AI features?

Yes. Project Boost Pro lists Ask AI and AI detection at $20 a month per project. Max lists Batch AI and unlimited AI detection at $200 a month per project. Read on Ahrefs' pricing page 9 November 2026.

Can I white-label an Ahrefs site audit?

Report branding was not stated on Ahrefs' pricing or Site Audit page on 9 November 2026. Ask Ahrefs about it before buying. Docket's paid version puts your own name and logo on the client PDF.

Can Docket replace Ahrefs?

For audits of public sites, if a local fix plan fits your work. Docket has no backlink or keyword index, rank tracking or Search Console connection. It has no shared workspace or always-on crawler, and its scheduled audits run only while the app is open and the computer is awake.
